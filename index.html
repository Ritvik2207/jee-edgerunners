Drop-year operating rules

Useful tips that compound

Small rules beat motivation spikes. Use these when the day feels messy, late, or heavy.

Repair before more mocks

After every mock, choose the three mistakes that cost the most marks and repair those before taking another test.

Chemistry needs recall

Keep a daily 20-minute NCERT and formula recall block. Chemistry improves faster when memory is revisited often.

Maths needs rough work

Do fewer questions neatly. Track where your solution broke: concept, algebra, speed, or question reading.

Physics needs diagrams

Draw the situation before formulas. Most mechanics and electricity mistakes start before calculation begins.

Protect the first block

Your first study block decides the tone. Start with a chapter you can actually finish or repair today.

Sunday is calibration

Use one weekly reset to update weak topics, mock scores, backlog, and next week's most important chapters.
